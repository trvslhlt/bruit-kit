CombFilterEffect

A short, audible-pitch-range delay line with feedback around it -- FlangerEffect's exact same wiring, but with the delay time fixed (tuned directly in Hz) instead of swept by an LFO. Feedback is bipolar: positive reinforces every harmonic of Frequency into peaks; negative reinforces the odd harmonics of half that frequency instead, turning Frequency itself into a notch. Try a held note at low Frequency with feedback near +/-0.9 for a resonant "ringing" tone.
